# My Favorite Series: Twinkling Watermelon (반짝이는 워터멜론)

![Twinkling Watermelon Poster](https://image.tmdb.org/t/p/original/bwTzW1wTgUxUOQruhT8DvinUYgR.jpg)

## About
**Twinkling Watermelon** is a *fantasy, romance, and coming-of-age* K-drama. Ha Eun-gyeol, a high schooler who is a CODA (a hearing child of deaf parents) with a gift for music, travels back to **1995**. There he meets the teenage version of his father and forms a band called **Watermelon Sugar**.

> A story about music, family, and finding a way to communicate without words.
