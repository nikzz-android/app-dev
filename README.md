# *Twinkling Watermelon*

The story follows Ha Eun-gyeol, a teenager who comes from a family of deaf parents. He's a talented guitarist who has a passion for music, but he struggles to balance his musical dreams with his responsibilities toward his family.

One day, he accidentally enters a mysterious music shop and finds himself transported back to 1995.

There, he meets a younger version of his father, Ha Yi-chan, who is still a high school student.

However, Eun-gyeol discovers something unexpected: his father is completely different from the person he knows, and his father's first love isn't the woman who eventually becomes his mother.

Eun-gyeol decides to get involved in his father's younger life, eventually forming a band called Watermelon Sugar with other teenagers.

As they spend time together, he discovers more about his parents' past, their struggles, their dreams, and the events that shaped his family.

The journey becomes much more than simply trying to bring his parents together. It's about understanding the people they were before they became his parents.
